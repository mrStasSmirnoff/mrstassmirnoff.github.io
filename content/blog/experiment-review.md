## Start with the decision

An experiment review should make the next action easier. I want to know what decision the team faced, what changed, and what evidence would have changed our mind.

## Keep the review compact

1. Decision and expected mechanism
2. Primary result and uncertainty
3. Guardrails and relevant segments
4. Known limitations
5. Recommendation and owner

The point is not to make every analysis look identical. It is to make the reasoning inspectable.
